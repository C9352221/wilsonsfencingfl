Our Story

Meet the Wilsons

Bill Wilson retired from the Manatee County Sheriff's Office in February 2022 after a career protecting the community. Right after that, Bill and Tina launched Wilson's Land Management Services LLC, now operating as Wilson's Fencing.

What started as a way to keep busy in retirement quickly grew into a full fencing company across central west Florida. The values are the same as they always were: do the work right, treat the customer like family, stand behind the result.

Our mission is simple. Keep your yards, pets, and kids safe. That's the whole job.
